On this piece

Island lighting usually means three pendants and a shadow on your knuckles wherever you chop. Tramway is one continuous extrusion with a sandblasted diffuser and a 96-CRI board, so the light lands flat across the whole worktop. The channel is drilled on a 1,600 mm centre so it can be mounted off two existing joists without a new box.
