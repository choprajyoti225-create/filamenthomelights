On this piece

Hung in a stairwell, Aubade is read from below and from the side at once, so the drop lengths are set on a musical interval rather than an even ladder — 340, 520, 610, 780, 900, 1180, 1460 millimetres. The eye never finds a repeat, which is the whole trick. Each globe is individually switched at the canopy so you can turn the cluster down to three lights for a quiet evening.
