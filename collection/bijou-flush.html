On this piece

Written for the corridors and box rooms that get whatever is left in the budget. Bijou is shallow enough for a 2.3 m ceiling and its diffuser is pressed rather than moulded, which removes the bright ring you normally get at the rim. It is the cheapest thing we make and the one we specify most often.
