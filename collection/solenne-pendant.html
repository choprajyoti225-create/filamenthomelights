On this piece

Solenne began as a rejected sample. The glassblower in Biot overshot the mould by four millimetres and the wall came out thicker at the base than the crown, which meant the light sank instead of spreading. We kept the mistake. That heavier base is why Solenne pools warmth onto a table rather than washing a whole room — it is a lamp for the length of a dinner, not for cleaning the floor.
