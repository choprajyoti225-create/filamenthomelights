On this piece

The shade sits at 1,240 mm — eye level for someone seated in a low mid-century chair, which is where most people read and where most floor lamps glare. Verrine tilts through 22° on a friction joint stiff enough to stay where you put it for a decade. The linen shade is stitched, not glued, and can be replaced without tools.
