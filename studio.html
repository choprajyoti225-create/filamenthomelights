House rules

Four things we will argue with you about.

  1. 01

    No room gets a single ceiling source

    Three layers minimum — something overhead, something at eye level, something low. It is the difference between a lit room and a used one.

  2. 02

    Nothing above 3000 K in a home

    Cooler light reads as clinical after dark. We will supply 4000 K for a workshop or a garage and nowhere else.

  3. 03

    Every circuit dims

    A dimmer is cheaper than a second fixture and does more work. We specify the driver and the dimmer together so they actually agree.

  4. 04

    Serviceable or it does not ship

    Replaceable lamps, replaceable drivers, stitched shades, unlacquered brass that ages instead of flaking.
