What a consultation involves

We would rather plan the light than sell you a lamp.

  1. 01

    A walk through the plans

    We start with your drawings, or photographs and a tape measure if there are no drawings. Ceiling heights, window orientation, where people actually sit.

  2. 02

    A lighting plan, room by room

    Layers rather than fittings: what you cook by, what you eat by, what stays on at midnight. Circuits and dimming get decided here, before anything is ordered.

  3. 03

    Fixtures made to the plan

    Drop lengths, finishes and colour temperature are cut to your ceiling. Everything ships in one crate with a wiring schedule your electrician can follow.
